# 13 — Demo tenant, synthetic corpus, and live replay

## 1. Principles
- The demo is a **synthetic tenant**, labeled once in the top bar. Everything in it is generated by code in `tools/`, reproducible from a seed.
- Real names of companies are never used. Vendors are named by **category** (e.g., "Protein Supplier"). The tenant name is neutral.
- The pipeline processes the synthetic documents for real (real LLM calls, real matching, real QBO sandbox posts), so every KPI is genuinely computed.

## 2. Tenant (`tools/seed/seed_demo.py`, deterministic seed 20260713)
- Org: **Northside Group**, slug `northside`, `is_demo=true`, timezone `America/New_York`.
- Entities (3): `Entity 1` → QuickBooks, `Entity 2` → QuickBooks, `Entity 3` (Commissary) → NetSuite (mock).
- Locations (34) with codes; the ones named in the design must exist: Riverside `RS`, Bayside `BS`, Commissary `CM`, Lakeshore `LS`, Old Mill `OM`, Pierhead `PH`, Saltbox `SB`, Canal Street `CS`; generate the remaining 26 with plausible neighborhood names and 2-letter codes (unique).
- Users: Elena Marsh `EM` (ap_manager), Tom Iwu `TI` (controller), plus 2 ap_clerks, and the demo viewer.
- Vendors (display names = categories): `Broadline Distributor`, `Protein Supplier`, `Produce Supplier`, `Janitorial Services` (two_way), `Equipment Supplier`, `Beverage Distributor`, `Dairy Supplier`, `Bakery Supplier`, `Paper Goods Supplier`, `Unlisted Supplier` (created 4 days before "today", bank mismatch vs W-9 — the fraud case; re-created with a fresh created_at by the replay each week so the story stays true).
- Items: ~180 SKUs across vendors with realistic descriptions and UoM (`Shrimp 16/20 IQF 2lb × 10`, `Cod loin 10lb case`, `Scallops U-10 dry 2lb`, `Crab claw meat 5lb`, `Oysters select 1gal`, `Romaine hearts 24ct`, `Heirloom tomato 10lb`, `Lemons 115ct case`, `Shallots 5lb bag`, `Flat-leaf parsley 60ct`, …). Prices: never round; cents distributed naturally.
- Contracts: Protein Supplier "Vendor agreement 2026", effective 2026-03-01 → 2026-09-30, countersigned 2026-03-03, clause **4.2** fixes `Shrimp 16/20 IQF` at **$3.68/lb**. Broadline and Produce have contracts covering ~60% of SKUs; Riverside's two newest suppliers have none (drives the Operations location note).
- GL accounts per `08` §4. Historical coded lines: 6 months of back-history (generated) so GL kNN has data.
- POs and GRNs generated per location per delivery schedule (produce daily, protein 3×/week, broadline 2×/week, janitorial monthly fixed).

## 3. Synthetic corpus generator (`tools/corpus`, Python)
Produces, for each invoice: the ground-truth JSON (canonical schema from `06` §5.1 without confidences) and one rendered artifact per channel:
- **Email PDF**: HTML → PDF (WeasyPrint) using 6 distinct supplier templates (different layouts, fonts, column orders, header positions, one with a table-less layout), realistic text layer.
- **Dock photo**: render PDF page → print-like degradation → photographic simulation: perspective warp (±8°), rotation (±4°), uneven lighting gradient, JPEG artifacts, slight motion blur, optional coffee ring / fold shadow / handwritten signature, and **controlled smudges** on chosen digits (to create genuine low-confidence cases). Output JPEG/HEIC-like JPEG.
- **Portal PDF**: a clean template served by `/demo/vendor-portal`.
- **EDI 810**: X12 text for Broadline Distributor only.
Label file `labels.jsonl` keyed by document sha256 → used by the eval harness. Commit the generator; generate the corpus in CI/infra (not committed, stored in Supabase Storage bucket `corpus`).

Target mix per day ≈ design proportions: email 59%, photo 18%, portal 14%, EDI 10%. Exceptions arise naturally from injected conditions at realistic rates: ~11% price variance (contract drift), ~4% qty mismatch (short deliveries), ~4% no PO (ad-hoc equipment buys), ~1% duplicates (same invoice via two channels), ~0.5% new-vendor fraud screens, ~2–3% smudged fields. Do **not** tune rates to hit the design's exact numbers; they will land near them.

## 5. Why the numbers will differ from the mocks
The mocks show illustrative values (214 today, 71% touchless, $3.10…). The live demo shows whatever the system computes. That is the point. Visual regression uses the fixture snapshot (`12` §4.1), never production.

## 6. Demo replay (WF-50 + `core/demo/replay.py`)
- Each night 04:00 org-local: generate "today's" schedule — ~180 documents (configurable, cap 400) with delivery-realistic timestamps between 05:30 and 21:00 (morning peak), assigned channels, and the 5 hero cases at fixed times (BD-104882 09:41:07, PS-88214 09:41, PR-2214 09:12:44, PR-2201 copies 08:02 / 16:44 on the day after its invoice date, NV-0001 ~09:00).
- Every 5 minutes: release due documents into the **real** channels: email via SES (send from a demo sender identity to the demo inbox — exercises SES inbound), photos via the capture upload API, portal PDFs published to the mock portal (picked up by WF-03), EDI files written to S3.
- Simulated humans: to keep the queues realistic (and "cleared" counts meaningful), a replay actor makes decisions on non-hero exceptions after a randomized delay (median 9 min, lognormal), using the recommendation when present. These decisions are made through the same RPCs as real users by a dedicated `ap_manager` user named `Demo replay`, so they are **visibly attributed** in every audit trail. They count toward decision-time and human-minutes KPIs using their simulated delay, and the Operations disclosure already states the tenant is synthetic. Hero exceptions are left open for the visitor to decide (the viewer role can't, so they remain open; Elena's seeded account can be used in a guided walkthrough mode — see §8).
- Nightly at 03:00: close out the day; exceptions older than 3 days in the demo are auto-resolved by the replay actor to prevent unbounded growth.

## 7. Reset
Monthly (1st, 02:00): archive and truncate demo org transactional tables (runs, events, invoices, exceptions, approvals, postings, costs), keep master data and eval history, re-seed back-history, void QBO sandbox bills created by the demo (script). Audit log for the demo org is archived to Storage as JSONL (+ its final hash) before truncation — the only sanctioned deletion path; implemented as a `security definer` function callable only by a dedicated maintenance role and itself audited in a separate `system_audit` table.

## 8. Guided walkthrough (optional, recommended for the portfolio)
A `?tour=1` mode for the demo viewer that overlays numbered, flush-left ink callouts (Modernist style, no rounded bubbles) walking through: pipeline → the smudged field → the shrimp variance → the duplicate → the fraud hold → operations. Actions in tour mode are simulated client-side only and never write data.
