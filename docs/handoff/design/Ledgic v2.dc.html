Ledgic
Accounts-payable automation for multi-location operators. Invoices arrive from email, dock photos, vendor portals and EDI; an LLM layer extracts every field with a confidence score; a matching engine checks every line against the purchase order and the goods receipt. Clean invoices post to the ERP, and only the exceptions reach a person.
