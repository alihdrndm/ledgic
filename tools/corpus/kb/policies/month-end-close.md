# Month-end close checklist (AP)

Owner: Controller. AP completes these steps by the third business day after month end.

## 1. Cut-off

1.1 Invoices received by 5 pm on the last business day are processed in the closing month.

1.2 Invoices dated in the closing month but received later are accrued, not posted, until the
following month opens.

## 2. Open exceptions

2.1 Clear or document every open exception older than 24 hours. Exceptions still open at cut-off are
listed in the close pack with the amount at risk and the reason.

2.2 Short-pays decided in the month are listed with the held amounts; held amounts stay in accounts
payable as disputed until the vendor's credit memo arrives.

## 3. Accruals

3.1 Goods received not invoiced (GRNI): accrue every GRN without a matched invoice at the PO price.

3.2 Reverse last month's GRNI accrual on the first day of the new month.

## 4. Reconciliations

4.1 Reconcile the AP subledger to the general ledger for each entity (QuickBooks for Entity 1 and
Entity 2, NetSuite for Entity 3).

4.2 Reconcile vendor statements for the top 10 vendors by spend; investigate any invoice on a
statement that is not in Ledgic.

4.3 Confirm that the Ledgic audit chain verifies ("Verify chain" on the Invoices screen) and attach the
result to the close pack.

## 5. Reporting

5.1 Report touchless rate, cost per invoice, held variances and duplicate/fraud catches from the
Operations screen. These figures are computed from the ledger; do not adjust them by hand.
