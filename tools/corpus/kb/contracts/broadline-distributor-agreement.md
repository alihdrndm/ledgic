# Distribution agreement 2026

Distribution agreement between **Northside Group** (the "Buyer") and **Broadline Distributor** (the
"Distributor") for dry goods, dairy, frozen, meat and disposables. Countersigned on {{COUNTERSIGNED_AT}}.

## 1. Term

1.1 Effective from {{EFFECTIVE_FROM}} through {{EFFECTIVE_TO}}.

1.2 Schedule A prices are fixed for the term. Items not on Schedule A are priced at the Distributor's
weekly list price as printed on the purchase order.

## 2. Ordering

2.1 Orders are placed through the Distributor's ordering portal by 2 pm for next-day delivery.

2.2 Every order carries a Buyer purchase order number; the Distributor's invoice must quote it.

2.3 Substitutions require the location manager's approval at the time of delivery and are invoiced at
the lower of the ordered and substituted item's price.

## 3. Delivery and receiving

3.1 Deliveries arrive between 6 am and 10 am. The receiving lead checks quantities against the
delivery manifest and records a goods received note (GRN).

3.2 Shorted items are recorded on the GRN and must not appear on the invoice. If they do, the Buyer
pays for the quantity received only.

## 4. Pricing and invoices

4.1 Prices include delivery. A fuel surcharge may be added only when diesel exceeds $5.00 per gallon
(national average) and must be a separate, labeled invoice line.

4.2 Invoices are issued per delivery and sent electronically. Payment terms are **Net 30** from the
invoice date. Multi-page invoices carry the
invoice number on every page.

4.3 Invoices above the Schedule A price are short-paid to the Schedule A price; the Distributor issues
a credit memo for the difference.

## 5. Rebates

5.1 A volume rebate of 1.5% applies to quarterly purchases above $150,000, paid as a credit memo within
30 days after quarter end.
