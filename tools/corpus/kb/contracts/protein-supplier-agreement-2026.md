# Vendor agreement 2026

Supply agreement between **Northside Group** (the "Buyer", on behalf of its operating entities and
locations) and **Protein Supplier LLC**, 1285 Terminal Way, New Bedford, MA 02740 (the "Supplier").
Countersigned by both parties on {{COUNTERSIGNED_AT}}.

## 1. Scope

1.1 The Supplier supplies fresh and frozen seafood and proteins to the Buyer's restaurant locations and
commissary against purchase orders issued from the Buyer's purchasing system.

1.2 This agreement governs every purchase order placed with the Supplier during the term, whether the
order is placed by email, phone or the Buyer's ordering portal.

## 2. Term

2.1 This agreement is effective from {{EFFECTIVE_FROM}} and runs through {{EFFECTIVE_TO}} (the "Term"),
when it ends unless renewed under 2.3 or terminated earlier under section 9.

2.2 Contract prices in section 4 are fixed for the whole Term. They do not change with market prices,
fuel costs or the Supplier's list prices.

2.3 Either party may propose a renewal in writing at least 30 days before the end of the Term.

## 3. Ordering and delivery

3.1 Every delivery must reference a valid Buyer purchase order number (format `PO-<location>-<number>`).

3.2 Deliveries are received at the dock by the location's receiving lead, who records quantities on a
goods received note (GRN). The GRN is the record of what was delivered.

3.3 Product delivered short, damaged or out of temperature is noted on the GRN and is not payable.

## 4. Pricing

### 4.1 Price basis

Prices are per unit of measure stated below, delivered to the Buyer's locations, including packaging,
delivery and fuel surcharges. No other charges may be added to an invoice.

### 4.2 Pricing: Shrimp 16/20 IQF

The contract price for Shrimp 16/20 IQF 2lb × 10 is **$3.68 per lb** for the Term. The price applies
to every location and every delivery, whatever the quantity ordered.

### 4.3 Pricing: Cod loin

The contract price for Cod loin 10lb case is **$68.40 per case (cs)** for the Term.

### 4.4 Pricing: Scallops

The contract price for Scallops U-10 dry 2lb is **$54.25 each (ea)** for the Term.

### 4.5 Pricing: Atlantic salmon

The contract price for Atlantic salmon fillet 10lb is **$117.83 per case (cs)** for the Term.

### 4.6 Price changes

A contract price can only change through a written amendment countersigned by the Buyer's controller.
A price increase notice, a new price list or a higher price printed on an invoice does not amend this
agreement.

### 4.7 Invoices above contract price

If an invoice bills a contracted item above its contract price, the Buyer pays the contract price and
holds the difference (a short-pay). The Buyer sends the Supplier a notice citing this section and the
relevant clause; the Supplier issues a credit memo for the held amount within 10 business days.

## 5. Invoicing and payment

5.1 One invoice per delivery, quoting the purchase order number, delivery date, item descriptions,
quantities, units of measure and unit prices.

5.2 Payment terms are **Net 14** from the invoice date, provided goods were received and the invoice
matches the purchase order, the GRN and this agreement.

5.3 Invoices are sent to ap@northside.example as PDF. Remittance is by ACH to the bank account on the
Supplier's W-9 on file. Changes to bank details follow section 8.

## 6. Quality and food safety

6.1 Frozen product must arrive at or below 0°F; fresh product at or below 38°F.

6.2 The Buyer may reject product that fails inspection at receiving; rejected product is not invoiced.

## 7. Disputes

7.1 The Buyer notifies the Supplier of any invoice dispute in writing within 30 days of the invoice date.

7.2 Undisputed amounts are paid on terms; only the disputed amount is held.

## 8. Remittance and bank details

8.1 The Supplier must notify bank account changes in writing on letterhead, signed by an officer.

8.2 The Buyer verifies every bank change by calling the Supplier's contact on file (not a number given
in the change request) before any payment is made to the new account.

## 9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this agreement with 60 days' written notice.

9.2 Contract prices apply to every delivery made before the termination date.
