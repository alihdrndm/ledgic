# Supply agreement 2026

Supply agreement between **Northside Group** (the "Buyer") and **Produce Supplier**, 664 Depot Ave,
Bronx, NY 10474 (the "Supplier") for fresh produce and herbs. Countersigned on {{COUNTERSIGNED_AT}}.

## 1. Term

1.1 Effective from {{EFFECTIVE_FROM}} through {{EFFECTIVE_TO}}.

1.2 Schedule A prices are reviewed quarterly. A revised Schedule A takes effect only once countersigned
by the Buyer's controller; until then the current Schedule A applies.

## 2. Delivery

2.1 Deliveries six days a week to each location before 9 am.

2.2 Drivers leave a paper invoice at the dock with every delivery. The receiving lead photographs it
with the Ledgic capture app and the photo is processed as the invoice of record. The Supplier also
emails a PDF copy; the Buyer pays each invoice once, whichever copy arrives first.

2.3 Product that fails quality inspection (temperature, ripeness, damage) is refused at the dock and
recorded on the goods received note.

## 3. Invoices and payment

3.1 Invoices quote the purchase order number and are payable **Net 14**.

3.2 Handwritten corrections on the dock invoice must be initialed by the driver and the receiving lead.
Uninitialed corrections are reviewed by AP before payment.

3.3 Weight-priced items are invoiced on delivered weight; case-priced items per case.

## 4. Pricing

4.1 Prices on Schedule A are delivered prices. Market-price items not on Schedule A are invoiced at
the price printed on the purchase order.

4.2 If an invoice bills a Schedule A item above its Schedule A price, the Buyer pays the Schedule A
price and holds the difference.
