# Vendor notes — Produce Supplier

## Account

Produce Supplier, Bronx, NY. Daily produce and herbs to every location. Terms Net 14.

## Dock invoices and photos

Drivers leave a paper invoice at the dock; receiving leads photograph it with the capture app. Photos
taken in a cold room are sometimes blurred, skewed or have wet smudges on handwritten quantities. Ledgic
sends these to extraction review when a quantity is hard to read — check the GRN quantity before
confirming.

## Duplicates

The Supplier also emails a PDF of every invoice, often the evening before the delivery. When the dock
photo arrives later the same invoice exists twice. Merge the copies and keep the earlier one; never pay
both.

## Pricing

Schedule A is reviewed quarterly. Market-priced items (berries, specialty herbs) follow the PO price.
