# Vendor notes — Protein Supplier

## Account

Protein Supplier LLC, New Bedford, MA. Seafood and proteins for all restaurant locations, mostly
Riverside, Bayside and the Commissary. AR contact: ar@protein-supplier.example. Terms Net 14. Contract:
Vendor agreement 2026 (shrimp, cod, scallops and salmon at fixed prices, see clauses 4.2–4.5).

## Recurring issue: shrimp billed above contract

The Supplier's billing system sometimes prints its current list price for Shrimp 16/20 IQF instead of
the contract price in clause 4.2. This happened twice this quarter; both times AP short-paid to the
contract price, sent a notice citing clause 4.2, and the Supplier accepted and issued a credit memo
within a week. Expect the same outcome: short-pay and notify, do not dispute the whole invoice.

## Receiving

Deliveries are usually complete. When a case is shorted the driver notes it on the delivery slip and
the GRN shows it; pay for the quantity received.

## Contacts

Billing questions go to the AR inbox above. Price amendments must come from the Supplier's sales
director in writing and be countersigned by our controller (clause 4.6) — an email from a sales rep is
not an amendment.
