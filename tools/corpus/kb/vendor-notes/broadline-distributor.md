# Vendor notes — Broadline Distributor

## Account

Broadline Distributor supplies dry goods, dairy, frozen, meat and disposables to every location under
the Distribution agreement 2026 (Schedule A prices). Terms Net 30. Invoices arrive by email as
multi-page PDFs, typically 10–15 lines.

## Matching

Broadline invoices are almost always touchless: they quote the PO, quantities match the GRN and prices
match Schedule A. When one does raise an exception it is usually a substitution (see agreement clause
2.3) — check the delivery manifest before deciding.

## Rebates

The quarterly volume rebate arrives as a credit memo; apply it against the next open invoice.
